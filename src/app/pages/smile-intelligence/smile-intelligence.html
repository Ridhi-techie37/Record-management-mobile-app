Score guide

What your Smile Score means

Your overall score is a number from 0 to 100:

  • 70+ — Good. Keep up the good habits.
  • 40–69 — Moderate. There’s room to improve.
  • Below 40 — Consider a dental check-up for advice.

How we calculate the Smile Score

We combine Alignment, Gum health, Whiteness, and Symmetry (each 0–100) with weights, then adjust for plaque risk (Low / Medium / High).

Confidence %

Confidence reflects how consistent your area scores are. This score is for guidance only and does not replace a professional dental check-up.
